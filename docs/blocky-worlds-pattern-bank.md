# Blocky Worlds pattern bank (style only)

Learned from the 38 reference videos in `reference/blocky-worlds/videos` (66–195 s, about 4.4 words a second).
They were transcribed locally with Whisper; the transcripts stay outside git. Only the shapes that made them work
are kept, in our own words: never a line, a name or a plot.

The source of truth is `supabase/functions/_shared/blockyWorlds/patternBank.js`. The writers read it as style notes:
- the hybrid planner and line writer;
- the dark planner and line writer;
- the idea pool, through `patternNotes(kind)`.

- **Hooks.**
  - The rule as plain fact in line 1.
  - "Except me" in line 2.
  - Or: loss first, mid-crisis then rewind, a hard number early.
- **Rules.**
  - "You see," then 2–4 everyday examples on ordinary jobs.
  - Neighbours paying a small, funny price.
  - One rule line said out loud.
  - The rule visible on screen (a number, line or light).
  - A status ladder with the narrator at the bottom.
- **Examples.** Sound words land the jokes; a small object planted early decides the ending; short shouted lines.
- **Twists.**
  - The rule's purpose is reversed.
  - The trusted adult betrays.
  - The narrator's lack wins.
  - The rule turns on the person at the top.
  - Someone is quietly replaced.
  - A new, worse rule as the sting.
- **Endings.**
  - A callback button.
  - An open question.
  - A comic reversal.
  - A heart reframe.
  - The final image shows the consequence.
- **Pace.** Line 1 within 2 s; the world explained by about 15–25%; then the pivot; most lines 5–9 words; the last line under 12 words.
