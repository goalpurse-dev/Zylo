# Overnight build 2, step 0: what the four reference videos do (methods only; every design of ours is our own)

1. Night city = thousands of tiny lit windows: every tower face is a grid of warm squares, about a third dark, randomly mixed; that alone carries the night.
2. The sky is deep saturated blue, never black; the towers read as dark shapes against it, windows as dots.
3. Light comes from below and from signs: street lamps make pools, shop fronts glow, the ground picks up warm reflections; faces are lit warm from the front.
4. Searchlight beams (white cones from the ground) cross the sky behind the giant in most big moments; haze makes them visible.
5. Camera mostly low and wide: on the street looking up the avenue, the towers leaning in (wide lens, low height), the vanishing point mid-frame.
6. Big moments use a near-ground fisheye looking straight up between towers, with the giant's head entering from the top.
7. Rooftop cameras look down at the street or level with the giant's face; the hero small at the edge of the roof.
8. The giant is tall and thin, pale, a flat face with a fixed grin; it moves slowly: walks between towers, bends down to street level, peeks into windows, covers its face to count.
9. The giant is always partly hidden (behind towers, cut by the frame) and lit from below; the face close-up fills the frame with glowing eyes.
10. Crowds: dozens of small people in the plaza all frozen, all looking up, then scattering; aerials show 50-100 tiny figures as dots.
11. Hiding: under the bed (two eyes in the dark gap), in closets, behind cars and shutters; one-room interiors lit warm with the city in the window.
12. Text boards: a giant scoreboard on a tower ("NIGHTS SURVIVED", "FOUND" with big numbers), billboards with one bold sentence, signs that change as the story counts.
13. Locations re-used: a clock tower with a gear room, a long bridge with lights, a parking lot, a fountain plaza, a subway entrance, a manhole that glows.
14. Pace: a new camera almost every word group (1-2 s); every shot has motion (a run, a turn, a push-in), nobody stands idle.
15. Colour: warm orange/yellow light against cold blue; red only for danger (flares, sirens); morning ends are bright and flat to show relief.

Not used from them: their monster's face or body, their billboard texts, their story (hide and seek with a counting giant), their characters, their names.
