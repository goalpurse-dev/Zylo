## How the ranking works

Every 2AM World starts from the same generator and the same six-scene format. What separates a strong entry from a weaker one is the reference itself — how sharp the contrast is between "how this world is normally shown" and "how it looks reframed at 2AM," and how instantly recognizable the reference is to a broad audience without extra context.
