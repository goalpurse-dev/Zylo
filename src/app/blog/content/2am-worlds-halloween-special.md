## Why Halloween and 2AM Worlds fit together naturally

2AM Worlds already leans on quiet, moody, slightly eerie atmosphere as its core visual language — which is most of what makes a Halloween scene work in the first place. Adding pumpkins, mist, and a full moon on top of that existing mood is a natural extension rather than a stretch.
