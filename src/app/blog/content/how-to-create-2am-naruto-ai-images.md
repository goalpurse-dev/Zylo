## Two ways to prompt a 2AM Naruto scene

There are two reliable starting points, and combining them usually produces the strongest result.

- **Location-first:** naming a specific place (the Hokage tower, the ninja academy, Ichiraku Ramen, the training grounds, the Hidden Leaf gates) gives Zyvo a clear physical space to build the scene around.
- **Character-first:** naming a character (Naruto, Sasuke, Kakashi, Hinata) helps Zyvo build the mood and framing around that character's world and tone.

## The 2AM Naruto prompt formula

**Character or location + specific place + time detail + lighting + mood**

### Location-based prompts

> 2AM at the Hokage tower, moonlight on the rooftop, the village asleep below, lantern light glowing from a single window, quiet responsibility.

> 2AM at Ichiraku Ramen, stools stacked and the sign switched off, steam still rising faintly from a closed kitchen, warm lamp light against a dark street, nostalgic stillness.

> 2AM at the ninja academy, empty classroom with a chalkboard still half-written, moonlight through tall windows, quiet memories of a long day of training.

> 2AM at the training grounds, three wooden posts under a full moon, scattered kunai marks in old bark, cool blue light and distant crickets, contemplative calm.

> 2AM at the Hidden Leaf gates, lanterns lit on either side, the road home empty and quiet, mist low across the ground, a sense of arriving somewhere familiar.

### Character-based prompts

> Naruto sitting alone on a rooftop at 2AM, orange jacket catching lantern light, the sleeping village behind him, quiet determination.

> Kakashi reading by lamplight at 2AM, a small apartment window overlooking the village, warm light against a cool night sky, calm solitude.

> Sasuke walking an empty village street at 2AM, moonlight and long shadows, quiet distance and unresolved thought.

- Name one character or one location per generation rather than several at once — a focused reference produces a more consistent six-image set than a crowded one.
