## How to customize these prompts

Before generating, change at least three details:

- The subject: student, traveler, worker, creature, siblings, or explorer
- The weather: rain, fog, snow, heat lightning, clear moonlight
- The era: ancient, 1980s, early 2000s, near future, timeless
- The key location: market, station, bedroom, harbor, tower, rooftop
- The emotion: comfort, wonder, homesickness, suspense, freedom

Keep the camera texture and lighting consistent across the sequence.

## Turn one prompt into a connected sequence

Choose one prompt, then plan six moments:

1. Establish the world.
2. Show an ordinary detail.
3. Introduce the subject.
4. Reveal the hidden location.
5. Pause for emotion.
6. End with the payoff.

Change only the action and location. Keep the world, era, subject, weather, lighting, camera texture, and emotion stable.

## FAQ

### What should I write in an AI world generator prompt?

Include the world, era, recurring subject, action, location, lighting, camera style, and desired emotion.

### Can I use these prompts exactly as written?

Yes. You will usually get a more distinctive result by changing the subject, location, weather, and emotional tone.

### Why do my generated scenes look unrelated?

The prompt may be changing too many core details. Repeat the same subject, era, lighting, weather, and camera texture while changing only the scene action and location.

### Are these prompts safe for commercial projects?

The concepts in this list are original, but generated output rights depend on the tool, model, plan, and applicable law. Review the relevant terms before commercial use.

### Which image format should I generate?

Use 9:16 for full-screen short-form content, 2:3 for Pinterest-first images, and 16:9 for blog heroes or YouTube thumbnails.
