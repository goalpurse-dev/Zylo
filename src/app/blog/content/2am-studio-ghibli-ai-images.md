## What makes an image read as "Ghibli-inspired"

Four choices separate this style from generic anime or cinematic AI art:

- **Painterly backgrounds.** Soft brushwork and natural texture rather than crisp digital linework — skies, grass, and water all feel hand-painted.
- **Warm naturalism.** Lighting leans toward golden lantern glow, soft dusk color, and gentle contrast rather than high-saturation neon.
- **A sense of scale and stillness.** Wide, quiet compositions where a small character exists inside a much larger, living world.
- **Everyday magic.** The wonder comes from ordinary details — a floating lantern, an old train, a quiet countryside path — not dramatic spectacle.

## The 2AM Ghibli prompt formula

**Painterly location + specific detail + light source + one small character detail + mood**

### Location ideas that read as recognizable

- A quiet countryside town with one window still lit
- A moss-covered train station platform, empty at night
- A bathhouse-style building glowing warmly against a dark sky
- A forest path lined with paper lanterns
- A rooftop garden overlooking a sleeping town

### Three ready-to-use prompts

> A quiet painterly countryside town at 2AM, Studio Ghibli-inspired soft brushwork, one lantern-lit window still open above a closed bakery, warm golden light against a deep blue night sky, gentle breeze in hand-painted grass, peaceful and nostalgic.

> An empty moss-covered train station platform at 2AM, painterly natural lighting, a single lantern swinging gently, distant hills fading into mist, quiet stillness and wonder.

> A warmly lit bathhouse-style building at 2AM, painterly background with soft steam rising, lanterns lining a stone path down to still water, golden light against deep indigo sky, cozy and a little magical.
