## 1. A coastal town at the end of summer

The arcade is closed, the road is wet, and the sea is almost invisible beyond the streetlights.

> A small coastal town after midnight during the final week of summer 1999, one teenager cycling past a closed arcade and quiet harbor, light rain, faded red signs, warm shop windows against deep blue shadows, candid 35mm photography, freedom mixed with the sadness of summer ending.

**Best final scene:** sunrise beginning as the cyclist reaches home.

**Best hidden detail:** moving weather maps asleep inside glass cases.

## 4. An empty shopping mall in 2004

Nostalgia comes from details: the food-court chairs, glossy tiles, silent escalator, and one machine still humming.

**Best opening text:** “You wake up here after everyone went home.”

## FAQ

### What does “a world at 2AM” mean?

It is a visual-story concept that imagines a real or fictional setting after most people have gone to sleep. The late-night setting creates quiet, mystery, comfort, or nostalgia.

### Which 2AM world should I generate first?

Choose a place with one personal emotional connection: a season you miss, a kind of world you always wanted to visit, or an ordinary location that feels different when empty.

### Do I need to name an existing franchise?

No. Original worlds give you more creative control and reduce the risk of implying affiliation with a brand or rights holder.

### How many scenes should a 2AM story include?

Six scenes work well for a carousel or short video: arrival, movement, detail, discovery, emotional pause, and final payoff.
