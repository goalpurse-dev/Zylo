## Why cyberpunk works so well at 2AM

Most 2AM Worlds take a bright, daytime-coded reference and reframe it as quiet and dark — that's the whole trick. Cyberpunk is the rare exception: the aesthetic is already built around neon-lit night streets, so there's no contrast to create. Instead, the appeal here is pure atmosphere — rain, reflection, and emptiness applied to a genre that's usually shown crowded and loud.

It also travels well because "cyberpunk city" doesn't depend on any single movie, game, or show — it's a recognizable visual genre on its own, which makes it one of the lowest-friction prompts in the entire 2AM Worlds catalog.

## The 2AM cyberpunk-city prompt formula

**Location style + specific detail + light source + one small sci-fi detail + mood**

### Location ideas that read as recognizable

- An empty neon-lit street at 2AM, rain-slicked pavement
- A rooftop overlooking a glowing skyline, steam rising nearby
- A narrow alley between towering neon-lit buildings
- A quiet elevated train platform, one train passing in the distance
- A rain-soaked crosswalk lit entirely by neon reflections

### Three ready-to-use prompts

> An empty cyberpunk city street at 2AM, pink and blue neon glow reflecting off wet pavement, steam rising from a street vent, tall futuristic skyscrapers fading into fog, quiet late-night stillness.

> A rooftop at 2AM overlooking a glowing cyberpunk skyline, rain falling softly, neon light scattered across the wet rooftop surface, a single figure-less silhouette of antennas and vents against the glow.

> A narrow alley at 2AM between two neon-lit cyberpunk buildings, puddles reflecting pink and cyan light, steam drifting from a vent, distant hum of a city that never fully sleeps.
