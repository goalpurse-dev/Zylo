# Zyvo Studio: Master Prompt v2 ("videos people actually watch")

Owner's brief, 2026-10-31. Condensed from the owner's message; the rules below are theirs.

## Why
First Studio posts flopped (186 views, 7.1 s average watch, 2% completion). The 5 "variants" were one video with a different text line. Two reference videos in the same 3D-cartoon style get thousands of likes: the difference is what the video is about and how it is told.

- **Reference A** ("Cheapest AI that's actually good?", 2:38, ~10k likes; described by the owner): opens on the viewer's problem, famous names and a money pain in 3 s; a headline question on screen ~10 s; pays it off step by step with useful info; a visual metaphor for every idea; one warm bright world, many angles; real screenshots BIG with a sticker callout; source stamps, admits what isn't proven; a confused kid (the viewer) + a calm mascot expert; value density beats length.
- **Reference B** (63 s, content/reference-videos/reports/): pain + name + number in 1 s; escalating numbers; 5 visible steps; mid twist; payoff at the end.
- **What ours did wrong:** about us, not the viewer; a cold open with no context; a dark empty studio, the creator's back to camera, tiny screens; the same footage 5 times; no information value.

## What the platforms reward
- TikTok (newsroom): interactions shape recommendations; finishing a longer video is a strong signal; follower count isn't direct; the feed avoids repetition.
- YouTube Shorts: a small seed audience first; the signal is viewed vs swiped away; re-uploads get reduced reach.
- Retention (industry, not official): strong hooks keep 80–90% past 3 s; 55–70% average watch is typical of winners; value-first beats intros; an early payoff (~15 s) plus a bigger one at the end; captions help; pauses over ~0.5 s hurt.
- Originality: templated near-identical videos get demoted. TikTok favours good videos over 1 minute: test 60–90 s too.
- Search: both read spoken words, on-screen text and captions: say and show the search phrase.

## Core rule
VIEWER PROBLEM → STORY → ZYVO AS THE ANSWER. Hook = headline question + relatable pain/curiosity + (when true) a famous name or number + an expressive character facing camera + a visual of the topic. Zyvo is never the hook.

## Angles (data; every video picks one)
A1 Trend teardown · A2 Famous-AI bridge (done for real, prompt and answer logged) · A3 Cheapest/fastest way · A4 Myth bust · A5 One-sentence challenge · A6 Tutorial in 60 s (search title) · A7 Idea list (save-bait) · A8 Story first (caption + end card only) · A9 Watch-along/reaction · A10 Things nobody tells you · A11 Honest fail · A12 Niche showdown.
Famous tools by name only in true statements; no logos, no implied partnership. Every number real and sourced or labeled "my estimate", with source stamps.

## Variety
A batch of 5 = 5 different videos: different angles, different main footage (a different creation each; different templates where possible), opening, headline, end trigger, world. Lengths mixed: at least one 20–35 s, one 60–90 s. Similarity check: over ~30% shared lines or main footage → rewrite. The same footage never twice within 14 days.

## Visual & animation bible
- Worlds (original, warm, bright; ≥ 4; ≥ 6 camera setups each): sunny diorama, cozy creator room, idea factory, a brighter Zyvo studio. No two videos in a batch share a main world.
- A visual metaphor prop for every line; a line with no visual is rejected.
- Characters face the camera, big expressions, never the back of the head for more than ~1 s. The creator asks the viewer's questions and objections; Zee is the calm expert with personality. Acting beats on key words.
- Real screens BIG: ~80–90% of the width, readable on a phone, one sticker callout, tight crops.
- Headline question at the top for the first ~6–10 s, then a small chip.
- Pacing: holds ≥ 2 s, moves 0.7–1.0 s, 170–190 wpm, new info every ~3–5 s, a visual change every 2–3 s, no dead air, overlays ≥ 2 s.
- 2–3 earned dopamine moments per video, each with a sound.
- Captions word by word, one key word coloured, ~65–70% height, never over faces or screens.
- Small source stamps on facts; estimates say so.

## Script structure
0–2 s hook · 2–6 s stakes/open loop · body: escalating answers with props, objections, a "tip most people skip" · early payoff within ~15 s, big payoff at the end · reveal (tryzyvo.com, link in bio) · ONE end trigger, rotated · loop.

## Voices
Two distinct, expressive voices; never clone or imitate a specific character or person. 3 candidates per character (< $0.20), the owner picks by ear, then locked.

## Learning
Each video stores angle, hook type, world, length bucket, famous name y/n, end trigger, template. Score = 40% 3 s hold/viewed rate + 30% average % watched + 20% (shares + saves) per view + 10% comments + likes. After every 10 posts with stats, a Studio playbook update. Then batches = 3 winning recipes (new topics and footage) + 2 experiments changing one variable. Retire an angle after 4 posts below the median.

## Let AI decide / prompt bar
5 angles, a template + reusable creation each, worlds, scripts, similarity check, plan + cost on ONE screen, render on Start. The prompt bar is the same, constrained to what was typed. Reuse first; generate new only with the owner's go and the cost shown.

## Hard rules
Real UI, real results, real numbers (or labeled estimates), no fake testimonials, no other companies' logos, no implied endorsements, no cloned voices, AI-content label on, budget guard on every paid call, ask before anything over $1. New voice lines ≤ $0.30 for the first batch. Deploy to laptop-transfer + the Content Machine functions, main untouched. Reply in 5 lines max.
