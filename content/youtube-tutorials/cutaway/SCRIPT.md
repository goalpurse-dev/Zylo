## Facts this script uses

- **proof_example**: A viral cutaway video by another creator and its real view count (the creator's public page, with the day you checked it: fill in [CREATOR] and [VIEWS])
- **starter**: Cutaway Explainer needs at least the Starter plan: €18 a month (VAT included) for 750 credits (the live plan-prices endpoint (2026-11-07) + cutaway/access.js (Starter and up generate))
- **premade_cost**: A 30-second video on a premade topic costs 25 credits; a custom topic costs 136 credits for 30 seconds or 195 for 60 seconds (cutaway/pricing.js PRICES.video.credits (the price shown on the Cutaway page))
- **per_month**: 750 credits ÷ 25 = up to 30 premade 30-second videos a month on Starter (worked out from the two facts above)
- **refund**: Charged once, when it starts; back in full if it fails (the Cutaway page's price line (CutawayBuilder.jsx))
- **premades**: The premade topics on the page: Landfill, French drain, Swimming pool, Car wash (the Cutaway page's PREMADE TOPICS chips (as recorded on screen))
- **lengths**: Premade topics are 30 seconds; custom topics 30 or 60 seconds (cutaway/pricing.js lengthsFor)
- **sfx**: Sound effects: digging, drips, engines and a chime on each name (the Cutaway page's sound effects switch)
- **how_it_works**: Script: a fact-checked script and an energetic voiceover. Scene: the 3D cutaway is built, and every part appears on its word. Render: one continuous vertical video with captions and sound. (the Cutaway page's How it works box)
